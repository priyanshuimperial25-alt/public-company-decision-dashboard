Model improvements included

The dashboard distinguishes reported revenue from management-adjusted revenue; uses a company-specific operating KPI instead of forcing “order book” onto every industry; separates current share price from filing-period financials; labels unavailable data; and uses transparent signal factors. For sectors with contracted work, enter backlog/book-to-bill. For platforms, enter NOV/GMV, orders or active customers. For banks, use NIM, credit cost and capital adequacy instead of EBITDA.
Data coverage is driven by the imported company record. This is a reusable dashboard, not a live market-data terminal. Refresh prices and results from linked primary sources before relying on a signal.
